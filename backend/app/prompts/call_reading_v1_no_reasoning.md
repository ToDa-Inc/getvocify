
## Final check before you answer (call_type)

Go through the decision order again, step by step, and stop at the first that fits:
- "no_conversation" only when the other side never really talked (voicemail, ringing, only "¿sí?/¿hola?").
- "not_a_sales_call" only with explicit evidence: someone says it is a test, it is a personal chat, or there is no prospect at all.
- "wrong_person" when the person says it is not their area, they left the company, or it is a wrong number — even if the talk went on afterwards.
- "bad_moment" ONLY when the prospect said they cannot talk now AND the call ended within a few turns with nothing else discussed. If they talked about the product, a meeting, an earlier contact or their situation, it is NOT bad_moment.
- A call about a meeting already set is "meeting_confirmation" / "meeting_reschedule", even if the prospect is busy.
- "follow_up" needs a sign in this call that this rep and this person spoke or wrote before; otherwise "cold_first_contact".

## Final check before you answer (ended_abruptly)

ended_abruptly is true ONLY when the line drops, the audio fails or the recording cuts in the middle of a sentence. It is false when the prospect says no, says goodbye, says they have to go, asks to be called later, or the rep wraps up — even if it was short or cold.
