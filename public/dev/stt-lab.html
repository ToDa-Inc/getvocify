STT lab: how long after you stop talking do the words finish?

Your microphone goes to the same live transcription (MAI) the app uses, as the other side of a call. The page watches your voice and the words that come back. For every sentence it measures, from the moment your voice stopped: when the first new words showed, when the last words changed, and when they settled. Speak a sentence, stop, wait about 3 seconds, and repeat 8 to 10 times. Everything stays in this page.
