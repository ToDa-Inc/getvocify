Mic as "them"

Plays your microphone into the Mac's system audio. The Vocify app takes the other side's voice from the system audio, so it hears you as the prospect: real capture, real transcription, real live help. Use it to test alone.

Wear headphones. If the sound comes out of the laptop speakers, the microphone hears itself and the sound screams. Also mute any music or videos: the app takes everything that plays on the Mac as "them".

How to test
  1. Put on headphones. Press Start here and say something: you should hear yourself with a tiny delay.
  2. In the Vocify app, start a meeting recording as you normally would, with live help on.
  3. Speak as the prospect (objections, long sentences, thinking pauses). The app shows your words as the other side.
  4. Your own microphone also goes to the app as "you". With headphones, that side stays quiet.
  5. When you finish, press Stop here.
